Volunteer Contribution Portal

About this project

PalGenoPedia documents incidents affecting civilian infrastructure — hospitals, universities, schools, and religious sites — each entry sourced, dated, and reviewed before publication. This portal is how approved volunteers add new, sourced incidents directly to the underlying records, instead of editing the spreadsheets by hand.

What happens when you sign in

  1. Pick a section below to work in.
  2. See every facility in that section with its current incident count.
  3. Open a facility and review its existing incidents, sorted by date — this is the duplicate check.
  4. Fill in the new incident: date, attack type, sourced account, casualties.
  5. Submit — it's added straight to the same sheet reviewers already use, with your account recorded as the submitter.
